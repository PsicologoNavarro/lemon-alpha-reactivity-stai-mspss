# Human confirmations required before v1.0.0

The ordered author names, affiliations, and ORCID identifiers in `CITATION.cff`, `.zenodo.json`, `CITATION.cff.template`, and `.zenodo.json.template` were populated from the final submission manuscript. No corresponding-author email was added because it is not required for software citation metadata. Before a public Zenodo release, the authors must still verify:

- the selected public GitHub destination, `PsicologoNavarro/lemon-alpha-reactivity-stai-mspss`;
- the populated author order, spelling, affiliations, and ORCID identifiers;
- copyright ownership and approval of the MIT license for original code;
- whether a verified grant identifier or Zenodo community should be encoded;
- institutional/data-governance confirmation for the planned public contents;
- eventual article DOI and relation only after the article DOI exists;
- authorization and date for publishing tag/release v1.0.0.

The manuscript funding statement, competing-interest declaration, and CRediT roles remain manuscript records; they were not converted into unverified Zenodo grant or contributor-role fields. The release-mode auditor now verifies that the active CFF and Zenodo files agree on author order, names, affiliations, ORCID identifiers, title, version, and license. It also requires a Git index containing every public file.
