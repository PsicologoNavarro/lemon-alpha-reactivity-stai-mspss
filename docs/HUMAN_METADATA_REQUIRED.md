# Release metadata status

The ordered author names, affiliations, and ORCID identifiers in `CITATION.cff`, `.zenodo.json`, `CITATION.cff.template`, and `.zenodo.json.template` were populated from the final submission manuscript. No corresponding-author email was added because it is not required for software citation metadata. Public release `v1.0.0` was authorized, published on 2026-09-07, and archived under version DOI `10.5281/zenodo.22647023`.

Items that remain optional or future-facing are:

- add a verified grant identifier or Zenodo community if the authors determine that one applies;
- add the article DOI and relation only after the article DOI exists;
- review author and license metadata again before every future release.

The manuscript funding statement, competing-interest declaration, and CRediT roles remain manuscript records; they were not converted into unverified Zenodo grant or contributor-role fields. The release-mode auditor verifies that the active CFF and Zenodo files agree on author order, names, affiliations, ORCID identifiers, title, version, and license. It also requires a Git index containing every public file.
